FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Sales Manager Training

What does a sales manager actually do?

A sales manager leads, coaches, and develops a team of sales representatives to achieve revenue goals. Unlike individual reps, a sales manager focuses on strategy, pipeline management, hiring, performance tracking, and motivating the team. They bridge the gap between company targets and frontline execution.

What is the best training for a sales manager?

The best training for a sales manager combines practical leadership skills with real-world coaching frameworks. Programs like those offered by RoofTop Sales go beyond theory — they equip managers with tools for pipeline management, team motivation, and performance tracking that can be applied immediately. Explore our training programs here.

What is the difference between a sales manager and a sales operations manager?

A sales manager focuses on leading reps, coaching performance, and hitting revenue targets. A sales operations manager works behind the scenes — managing tools, processes, data analytics, territory planning, and compensation structures. Both roles are essential, and the strongest teams invest in training for each.

How long does sales leadership training take?

Sales leadership training programs vary in length. Short workshops run two to three days, while comprehensive sales manager training programs span several weeks with ongoing reinforcement. At RoofTop Sales, we believe real behavior change requires consistent practice, which is why our programs include post-training support. Get in touch to learn more.

Why do top sales reps fail as managers?

Selling and managing are fundamentally different skill sets. A top rep excels at closing deals, handling objections, and building client relationships. But a sales manager needs to coach, motivate, delegate, and think strategically. Without proper sales manager training, even star performers struggle to lead teams effectively.
