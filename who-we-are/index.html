OUR STORY

Sales Development That Puts People First

RoofTop Sales is a sales training, coaching and consulting company based in Dubai, working with businesses and sales professionals across the UAE. We bring practical selling skills, leadership development and customer-focused thinking into the way teams work, helping people build stronger conversations, better habits and more confident performance.

From frontline sales teams to business leaders, our work is shaped around the real demands of each organisation. We focus on relevant training, thoughtful coaching and practical tools that teams can carry into their everyday customer interactions.

OUR PROMISE

Our Promise

Selling is harder than ever. Building trust and influencing decisions has never been more challenging.

Our mission is simple: help frontline sales teams learn a better way to break through buyer resistance, become trusted advisors, and develop the confidence to lead every conversation.

Our goal is to replace outdated pressure tactics with a genuine passion for solving customer problems. Buyers are tired of being sold to. Our role is to restore their belief that salespeople can truly help them succeed.

Our mission is to represent your customer. If you understand them, you can serve them. If you serve them, your numbers grow and your team loves what they do.

FAQ

Asked Before the First Call

What is the difference between coaching and training?

Training teaches a skill to a group, in a structure. Coaching stays with a person, on their live deals, and corrects the work in real time. Our programmes use both. A workshop without coaching rarely changes a month.

Do you work with small companies?

Yes. Founders and small teams use the CEO programme, the mastermind, and Entrepreneur Connect. A large corporate uses the executive, manager, and channel programmes. The design changes with the size. The standard does not.

Which industries?

Real estate, technology, financial services, retail, manufacturing, FMCG, and professional services are the ones we are asked for most. The method is adapted after we understand your market. We do not pretend a property sale and a software sale are the same conversation.

Can it be online?

Yes. In person across the UAE, live online, or a mix. Online sessions stay interactive. They are not a recording with a quiz at the end.

How do we start?

Write or call. The first conversation is to see if the problem is one we should take. If it is, we outline a path. If it is not, we will say so.
