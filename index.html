Questions

Before you book a conversation.

What makes Rooftop Sales different from other sales training?

Most sales training leans on scripts and pressure. We teach people to build trust and to understand how a buyer actually decides. If the prospect does not feel heard, the pitch will not land. The work is to change the conversation, including with buyers who have stopped listening.

How do you measure whether the training worked?

We watch four things: mindset, sales activity, skill, and revenue. If results stall, that view shows where the team needs a different kind of support, and we adjust.

How long does it take to see results?

A workshop can shift how people think straight away. Lasting behaviour usually takes three to six months. We stay through that stretch. Training is not a single day and then silence.

Do you customise the programme?

Yes. Each programme is shaped around your industry, your sales process, the way the team is built, and the goal. We keep what already works and close the specific gaps holding the number back.

What will the team actually learn?

How to earn trust before pitching. How to ask until the real need is clear. How to talk about value in a way that matters to that buyer. How to move a deal forward without pressure, and how to handle an objection by listening rather than pushing harder.
