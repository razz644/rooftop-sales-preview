The idea

The pitch is rarely the whole problem.

Pitch to Profit is how Rooftop Sales opens the door. There is no standing date published on this site. Tell us you want a seat and we will send the next session directly. If a public date is running, it will be in that note, not buried in a banner.

Many sellers respond to a lost deal by adding more slides, more features, and more talking. The webinar looks at the other side of the meeting: what the buyer needs to say, and what the seller needs to hear, before a presentation can land.

A shift in approach

From presenting at buyers to talking with them

When the pitch does the work

  • The seller talks most of the meeting
  • The deck is the same for every buyer
  • Questions arrive at the end, if at all

When the conversation does the work

  • The buyer explains the problem in their own words
  • The presentation answers what was actually said
  • Both sides know the next step before they leave
