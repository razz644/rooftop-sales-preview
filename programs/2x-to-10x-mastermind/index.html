What it is

A year in a room of owners, with a coach who stays on the number.

This is not a two-day workshop. Places are limited. Members meet monthly, take private coaching on their own business, and review the plan through the year. You bring the real number. The group and the coach stay on it.

The mastermind is built for a specific moment in a business: sales are coming in, but growth has slowed to a rate the founder is not happy with. Working harder on the same approach rarely changes that. A clear plan, honest peers, and regular review usually do.

The thinking

Why sales growth needs a system

When growth depends on the founder’s own effort, it stops when the founder’s time runs out. A system lets the business keep selling while the owner leads.

01

A plan you can measure

Growth starts with the real number on the table and a plan that says how it moves.

02

A process the team can run

Sales systems and the right hires let the business sell without every deal going through the founder.

03

Review that keeps it honest

Peers and a coach who look at the plan regularly make it hard for good intentions to drift.

Who it is for

Who should consider the mastermind

This room works best for owners who already have a business that sells, and want an honest group around the next stage of growth.

  • CEOs and founders with existing revenue who want a faster rate
  • Owners ready to be in a room where the number is discussed plainly
  • Leaders who will implement, not only attend

The format

How the programme works

  1. Bring the real number

    You join with your business as it is today. The group and the coach work from your actual figures.

  2. Meet the group every month

    Monthly mastermind sessions with other growth-minded CEOs, where the number is discussed plainly.

  3. Take private coaching

    One-to-one strategy calls on your own business, between the group sessions.

  4. Review the plan through the year

    Templates and a quarterly review keep the plan current for the full twelve months.

Format: 12 months, with monthly group sessions and individual coaching. Ask us about the next intake. Places are limited.
