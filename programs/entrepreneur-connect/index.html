Why it exists

A room built on referrals, not another panel.

The first meeting was on 19 September 2020. The community’s own record is more than 325 regular members, and more than 3,000 entrepreneurs who have joined a session. A typical morning moves through introductions, referrals, a business presentation, and a close. Speakers join from time to time. Alifiya Sura leads the room.

Business by introduction depends on trust, and trust needs time with the same people. A fixed weekly slot gives members the chance to know each other’s work well enough to recommend it.
